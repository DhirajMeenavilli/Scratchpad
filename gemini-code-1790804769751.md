# McDonald's Corporation (MCD) — Comprehensive Economic Valuation & Thesis

This report documents the step-by-step revaluation of McDonald's Corporation from an accounting asset/liability framework to a dynamic **Net Asset Value (NAV)** model, incorporates multi-year capital deployment, constructs a valuation scenario framework (Bull, Base, Bear), and addresses current market pricing dynamics.

## 2. Liabilities & Equity Reconciliation (t=0)

Liabilities were adjusted to account for macroeconomic yield environments and contractual obligations:

* **Long-Term Debt:** $41.500B *(Reported: $39.973B; adjusted with buffer for yield/refinancing risks)*.
* **Lease Liabilities:** $15.500B *(Reported: $14.841B; total obligations to master landlords)*.
* **Current & Operating Liabilities:** $6.492B *(Accounts payable, accrued taxes, deferred fees at face value)*.
* **Total Adjusted Liabilities:** **~$63.492 Billion**.

### Net Economic Equity Calculation
Subtracting adjusted liabilities from adjusted assets replaces the accounting equity deficit:

$$\text{Net Economic Equity (NAV)} = \$258.820\text{B} - \$63.492\text{B} = \mathbf{\$195.328\text{ Billion}}$$

Dividing by **710.4 million shares outstanding** yields a baseline NAV of approximately **$274.95 per share**.

*Note on Accounting Equity Lines:* Retained Earnings ($70.3B), Additional Paid-In Capital ($9.6B), and Treasury Stock (-$79.3B) are historical source/use entries and are excluded from the NAV calculation to avoid double counting.

## 5. Synthesis & Investment Thesis

* **Current Market Pricing Context (~$230/share):** The stock currently trades near a low-end/bearish weighting rather than its probability-weighted baseline (~$260–$270). This gap reflects short-term market fears around low-income consumer traffic slowdowns, value-menu margin friction, franchisee capital expenditure burdens, and multiple compression to ~18x–19x forward earnings.
* **Core Long-Term Thesis:** McDonald's functions as a high-quality, defensive, income-generating compounder. It is not a deep-value arbitrage or hyper-growth "outperformance" play, but offers an expected long-term total annualized return of **~8% to 9%** (comprising a ~3.3% dividend yield + 4%–6% EPS growth).
* **Volatility Profile:** While holding periods over 20–30 years smooth out valuation swings and market drawdowns, single-stock concentration risk remains. Investor expectations should be set around steady, low-beta compounding rather than market-crushing alpha.
